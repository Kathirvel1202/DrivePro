01 · GETTING STARTED

Before your first lesson

Confirm permit and identification requirements with your local authority and school. Share your experience level, preferred vehicle type, and questions before the lesson begins.

Ask DrivePro a question →

02 · ROAD AWARENESS

Build a steady observation routine

Ask your instructor how to scan mirrors, check blind spots, and notice pedestrians, cyclists, and changing traffic. Local road rules always take priority.

03 · PRACTICE

Make each practice session count

Choose a clear focus with your instructor, practise safely in an appropriate setting, and review what felt comfortable and what needs more guidance. Never practise beyond your permit conditions.
