WHAT YOU NEED TO ENROLL

License Eligibility & Document Requirements

Ensure you have the proper identification and permits ready before scheduling your first in-car lesson.

01 · AGE & IDENTITY

Minimum Age & Identification

You must be at least 16 years old for Class D/M learner permits (17 for practical road tests in certain jurisdictions). Bring a government photo ID or passport.

02 · LEARNER PERMIT

Valid Provisional / Learner Permit

You must hold a valid state or national provisional learner permit to drive on public roads. We verify your permit details prior to your first lesson.

03 · EYESIGHT CHECK

Visual Standard

You must be able to read a standard vehicle number plate from a distance of 20 meters (with glasses or contact lenses if normally worn).
