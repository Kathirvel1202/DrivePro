STEP-BY-STEP READINESS

Your Driving License Acquisition Checklist

Know exactly what is required at every milestone so you can prepare your documents and training with confidence.

01

Eligibility & Permit

Verify you meet the minimum age requirement (16+ / 17+) and apply for your official Learner/Provisional Driver Permit with proof of identity and vision check.

02

Theory & Perception

Pass your official Theory and Hazard Perception test. We provide complimentary practice question banks and highway code study guides.

03

In-Car Driving Hours

Complete your scheduled dual-control lessons with your assigned instructor, tracking your progress across steering, parking, junctions, and speeds.

04

Official Practical Exam

Your instructor accompanies you to the testing center in the dual-control training car you mastered. Pass with pride and claim your full driver's license!
