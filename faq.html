The Practical Exam & Mock Tests

Everything about test day, mock simulations, and school vehicle rental.

Yes! Official test-day car hire is included FREE in our 20-Hour Standard and 30-Hour Complete packages. Your instructor will pick you up, conduct a 45-minute warm-up drive to calm your nerves, accompany you to the testing center, and wait while you take your test.

A mock test is a full 45-minute simulation conducted under strict exam conditions along real examiner routes. Your instructor uses the official grading sheet, marking minor and serious faults, followed by an in-depth debrief to fix any remaining issues before the real exam.
