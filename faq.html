Course and eligibility

Course and eligibility

Find out what to ask about eligibility, documents, and vehicle categories.

Start by identifying the vehicle category and asking the school about eligibility, course scope, and available instruction.

Requirements depend on your location and circumstances. Confirm the current list with the school and licensing authority.

Ask the school and licensing authority which steps, assessments, and fees apply to changing or adding a vehicle category.

Lessons and scheduling

Lessons and scheduling

Ask the school how lessons are scheduled, rescheduled, and confirmed.

Ask the school about available time slots, lesson duration, confirmation, and rescheduling policies.

Check the school’s cancellation and rescheduling terms before booking.

Lesson length can vary by school and course. Confirm the session duration and how much time is spent driving before you book.

Training and assessment

Training and assessment

Ask what the training covers and how progress is reviewed.

Training commonly covers vehicle controls, observation, positioning, hazard awareness, and responsible road use.

Ask how instructors provide feedback and record completed practice.

Ask about instructor availability, additional-session rates, and whether extra practice affects your course plan.

Fees and payments

Fees and payments

Confirm current fees, included lessons, payment timing, and refund policies directly with the school.

Fees vary by school and course. Request a current fee breakdown and confirm what is included.

Refund terms depend on the school’s written policy. Review the terms before paying.

Payment plans differ by school and package. Ask whether instalments are available and whether any fees or conditions apply.
