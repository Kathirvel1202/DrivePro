GETTING STARTED

License Eligibility & Permits

What you need before getting behind the wheel for your first in-car lesson.

You can begin taking driving lessons as soon as you turn 16 (or 17 depending on your state/regional licensing jurisdiction) and have been issued a valid provisional or learner permit. You do not need to wait until your practical test date to start learning.
