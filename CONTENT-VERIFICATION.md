# Instructor and testimonial content review

## Public page changes

- Instructor profile cards now use a consistent pending-verification treatment. Names and portraits remain visible for the owner to confirm; specialties, vehicles, experience, and credentials are marked pending or hidden.
- Instructor portrait paths were checked and all six files exist under `asset/images/instructors/`. The image source manifest does not identify these six portraits or confirm that the pictured people match the displayed names. Alt text therefore describes them as portraits whose identities are pending confirmation.
- Profile ratings, review counts, pass-rate figures, and the unsupported licensing/background-check guarantees are no longer shown on the instructor pages.
- The homepage review quotes are hidden and replaced with a note that source, wording, and publication permission must be verified.
- The homepage metric strip is hidden pending business records. The 98.4% claim was also removed from the About and Instructor hero messaging.
- Profile inquiry links still point to `contact.html#book` with the instructor query parameter. The booking script matches those names against the corresponding contact-form options.

The admin dashboard, instructor roster, reports, and instructor portal display figures and records that are not live or verified business records. These pages include instructor credentials, ratings, and a 98.4% pass-rate figure (including an unsupported comparison with a 48.2% national average). Do not reuse these as verified business records or public claims. Other portal pages also contain sample transactions and learner records; replace them with verified operational records before use.

Business information remains unverified. The contact page no longer publishes the sample phone, email, address, hours, map, or guarantee. Its booking form does not send or persist submissions without a configured backend.
