THE 6 CORE MODULES

Curriculum Breakdown: What You Will Practice

Our syllabus follows official State Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV) testing standards.

01

Cockpit Drill & Controls

Proper seating ergonomics, head restraint, steering wheel distance, mirror angles, blind spots, foot pedals, and instrument cluster warning lights.

02

Moving Off & Stopping

Clutch biting point, smooth accelerator balance, 6-point observation routine, hill starts without roll-back, and controlled progressive braking.

03

Junctions & Roundabouts

The MSPSL routine (Mirrors, Signal, Position, Speed, Look), approaching T-junctions, cross-roads, mini-roundabouts, and multi-lane gyratories.

04

The 4 Test Maneuvers

Parallel parking behind a parked car, reverse bay parking, drive-in forward bay parking, pulling up on the right side, and emergency stopping.

05

Highways & Night Driving

Joining 70mph dual carriageways via slip roads, maintaining the 2-second safe space cushion, overtaking safely, and night-time glare management.

06

Mock Test Simulation
