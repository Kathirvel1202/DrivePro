BOOKING QUESTIONS

Common Questions About Scheduling Lessons

Quick answers to help you book with complete confidence.

Your certified instructor will drive to your home, workplace, school, or university campus at the scheduled time. Your lesson begins right from your pickup point, and you are dropped back off at the same location at no additional cost.

Please have your plastic provisional or learner driving permit card ready. If you wear corrective glasses or contact lenses for driving, please wear them. We take care of vehicle insurance, dual controls, and fuel.

You can reschedule any lesson free of charge by calling or texting our admissions desk at (800) 555-0199 or notifying your instructor directly at least 24 hours prior to the scheduled session.
